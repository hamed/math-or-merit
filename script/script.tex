\subsection{The crowd}\label{crowd}

People come in from both sides, hopping, and settle as a loose crowd —
truly random, no rows, no clusters of one shape or colour. They start a
little different: one, two or three coins each. While the title builds they
chat in pairs, nod, laugh now and then, and look up at each new word and at
the spinning reel. Then 27 coins drop straight down from the title, from
every place a letter stands flat on its line — M and h two each, r and i
one, none from e, o or a; three from each place — falling under one
gravity. Each lands on whoever is under it, and a bigger circle is a bigger
target: the more you hold, the more you catch. They hop over to catch them;
now and then a neighbour goes for one too and arrives late. One who started
with a single coin ends with 15; one catches nothing and keeps the one. The
rest hop out. Those two stay: the big one under MERIT, the small one under
MATH. No words, no numbers.
