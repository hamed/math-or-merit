\section{Effective participants}

How many equal fortunes would be just as concentrated as this room.
Everyone equal: all of them. One owner: 1. No money: you don't count.
Open the black box: write each person's share of the room as $s_i$. Square the shares, add them, then take the reciprocal:
\todo{draft}

\[ \frac{1}{\sum_i s_i^2} \]

This is the inverse Herfindahl concentration index.
