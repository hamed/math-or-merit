# Research backlog

Prioritized audit of unresolved research requests already present in `outline.md`,
`ideas.md`, and the research memos. This is the queue for evidence work, not a feature
roadmap.

## Evidence modes

- **Literature** - a published result, primary source, standard, or authoritative data.
- **Source** - provenance, context, exact wording, date, and reuse rights for narrative
  or cultural material.
- **Experiment** - a preregistered local simulation or statistical fit.
- **Reader test** - evidence that an ordinary reader understands a visual or interaction.
- **Model decision** - an exact mechanism must be defined before evidence can answer the
  question.

An item is complete only when its owning memo records a verdict and essay-safe wording,
or explicitly rejects the proposed claim.

## First review-pass corrections

- Do not search for one canonical spherical-cow script; published and oral variants differ.
- Do not say a log plot reveals a power law. A fit, goodness-of-fit test, and comparison
  with alternatives are required.
- Do not use radius proportional to wealth while describing circle size as an honest
  quantitative encoding; that makes area proportional to wealth squared.
- The Bianconi-Barabasi network-condensation paper is not a Yard-Sale or asset-exchange
  result. A separate 2005 Bianconi-Marsili coauthored paper concerns scale-free networks
  with exponent below two; it does not establish the proposed wealth/Bose-Einstein chain.
- The intervention code implements periodic wealth levies and equal dividends. Published
  Fokker-Planck redistribution results cannot be imported without matching mechanisms.
- Do not treat repeated stable windows from one room as independent experiments.
- Do not count levy or dividend transfers as ordinary trade turnover.
- Scalar rank mobility is deferred until the horizon and near-zero rank-churn problem are
  resolved.
