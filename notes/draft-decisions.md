## D6 — "You are your net worth" stays authored; no attribution exists — **decided (2026-06-23)**

- **Search result (web, 2026-06-23):** no famous quote earnestly asserts "you are your net
  worth." The well-known ones **invert** it — Suze Orman ("your self-worth is not your
  net-worth"), Marcus Aurelius (*Meditations*, "a person's worth is measured by the worth of
  what he values"), and *Fight Club* ("you are not how much money you have in the bank").
- **Decision:** keep "You are your net worth" as an **authored, deliberately-crude** line.
  Its wrongness is the point. **Do not attribute it to anyone.**
- **DIRECTION CORRECTED (2026-06-23):** the wanted quote is the *cynical/earnest* one —
  something that asserts you ARE your money / your net worth — NOT the inversion. The famous
  inversions (Fight Club, Orman, Aurelius) are therefore the wrong polarity for this beat.
- **Earnest "you are money" candidates (from memory — verify wording/attribution before
  shipping, per D1):**
  - **Jay-Z** — *"I'm not a businessman — I'm a business, man."* Closest to identity = money.
    Recommended.
  - **Gordon Gekko**, *Wall Street* — *"Greed, for lack of a better word, is good."* Iconic
    but about greed, not worth-as-identity.
  - **Ayn Rand**, *Atlas Shrugged* (Francisco's money speech) — *"Money is the barometer of a
    society's virtue."* On-theme but long and ideologically loaded.
- **RESOLVED (2026-06-23):** prose uses the authored, unattributed line **"You are your
  money."** Chosen over "You are your net worth" because *net worth* is finance jargon; the
  simpler phrasing reads instantly for non-native / mobile readers and keeps the same crude
  punch. No external quote is used — none fits "close and simple" without baggage or
  attribution risk. Sourced epigraph idea (Jay-Z etc.) dropped.

## D8 — Writing stance: narration is voiceover to a stage (architecture NOT fixed) — **stance (2026-06-23)**

- **What this is:** a *writing* stance, not an architecture decision. The user explicitly does
  not want the presentation model fixed yet; the prose should be written AWARE of it instead.
- **The model (informal):** think in LOCATIONS. One location is a canvas/stage that holds the
  picture in a fixed place and morphs there — it does not scroll with the text. Another
  location is where text arrives, sits, and leaves in chunks. Content flows through fixed
  places (a stage), rather than a column scrolling past a pinned graphic.
- **How the words change:** write narration as voiceover — lean on the canvas (deixis,
  present-tense morph, continuity like "the same circle the cow became"), don't describe what
  the canvas already shows, and never say "the canvas" to the reader. Per-beat canvas intent is
  noted inline as `CANVAS →`.
- **Through-line (opening arc), now in prose:** ONE canvas morphs cow → sphere → uphill →
  (same place) trillionaire cartoon → strip detail → circle → grows with coins → multiplies
  into the room. The three old inline `<figure>`s are collapsed into one `stage` figure + cues.
- **The "break":** the authored morph can hand the stage to an interactive widget (the sim).
  Same place, new occupant; different replay contract (you can't un-run a stochastic sim by
  scrolling up). Named so it is not an accident.
- **NOT decided (deliberately):** whether the beat is a unit of *authoring* or only of
  *presentation* (adjacent to ADR-004); whether to write ADR 013 later. Revisit only when
  something needs to lean on the seam in code.
- **ADRs amended (2026-06-23, non-overturning):** ADR-004 carries an Amendment flagging the
  prose-dominant / inline-manifest tension and the authoring-vs-presentation hinge; ADR-005
  carries an Amendment extending the no-special-casing guard to any future stage/timeline
  sequencer. Both keep their decisions; neither is reopened yet.
- **Tone watch:** a persistent canvas with transient text is slightly less "prose-dominant"
  than AGENTS.md states — keep the meaning in the words even when they are visually transient.
- **Spike planned:** the empirical follow-up — which engine drives the stage, and how it
  coexists with the sim — is written up in [`presentation-spike.md`](presentation-spike.md)
  (two approaches A/B, two phases, asset + provenance plan, comparison criteria). Not built yet.
