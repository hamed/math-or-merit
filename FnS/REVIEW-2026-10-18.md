## New stage findings

### 1 — P2: a triangle or hexagon gains area when a trade cuts it

Locations: [piece.ts:27](stage/acts/piece.ts#L27), [track.ts:150](stage/acts/track.ts#L150), [render.ts:258](stage/view/render.ts#L258).

`Live.box()` supplies a shape's bounding rectangle. `Money.mark()` subtracts the stake from that rectangle, and the renderer draws the remainder as `RECT` whenever a display override exists. A triangle's bounding rectangle has twice its actual area; a hexagon's is also larger than the shape. The override therefore introduces area that was never wealth.

Reproduce with `new Session({ n: 2, seed: 1 })`, run `tri; finish; trade 1`, then advance and draw 60 frames at `1/120` second. At act age 0.5 seconds:

| Shape | Winner area | Losing body area | Detached stake | Total drawn area |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Square control | 0.5 | 0.4 | 0.1 | 1.0 |
| Triangle | 0.5 | **0.9** | 0.1 | **1.5** |
| Hexagon | 0.5 | **0.5666667** | 0.1 | **1.1666667** |

This is during the lift, before docking; trails are excluded. The losing triangle abruptly becomes a rectangle, rather than losing the pictured slice. Opacity does not account for the override either: `StageView.body()` obtains density from the stage's underlying body.

Make cuts aware of the actual shape, or disable these acts for unsupported shapes until an area-preserving conversion is available. Add a regression that measures the rendered remainder and detached piece for every supported shape, rather than testing only the ledger.

### 2 — P2: overlapping trades overwrite each other's cuts but retain all pieces

Locations: [transit.ts:77](stage/core/transit.ts#L77), [transit.ts:160](stage/core/transit.ts#L160), [track.ts:140](stage/acts/track.ts#L140), [track.ts:170](stage/acts/track.ts#L170).

Transit admits several trades involving the same holder concurrently. Each act captures a source box and later writes its remainder to `scene.display`. Since that map has one entry per holder, the last act replaces the other cuts while all detached pieces remain in `scene.pieces`.

Minimal setup, with `Session` and `emptyScene` imported from their owning modules:

```ts
const s = new Session({ n: 2, seed: 1 });
s.run('rule pairing random; play 10');
for (let frame = 0; frame < 117; frame++) {
  s.frame(1 / 120);
  const scene = emptyScene();
  s.transit.draw(s.live, scene);
  // Inspect this scene's body overrides and solid pieces.
}
```

At frame 117, body areas total **0.84736** and solid pieces total **0.42864**. Even taking the exact geometric union of those rectangles, removing overlap and excluding trails, leaves **1.0677541177** units of visible area for total wealth 1. None of these acts has reached docking. Meanwhile the ledger correctly reports shown wealth **0.724** plus transit wealth **0.276**: the accounting test passes while the geometry disagrees.

Serialize acts that share holders, or compose all active changes into one authoritative geometry per holder. A rejection by the animation scheduler must also defer the game trade, rather than applying it unseen. Test streamed random trades with a small population and compare the scene with the ledger.

### 3 — P2: free-room recipes return before calculating the required walls

Location: [figure.ts:191](stage/core/figure.ts#L191), compared with the extent calculation at [figure.ts:275](stage/core/figure.ts#L275).

The `free` branch calls `spreadOut()` or `spreadPairs()` and returns with `right` and `top` still equal to 2. Other placed poses calculate their actual extents before returning.

```ts
const wealth = Float64Array.of(0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4);
const figure = compile(parseRecipe('bar 0.01 free'), wealth);
const stage = new Stage(figure, wealth);
```

This valid recipe creates widths `[10, 20, 30, 40]`, but `figure.right` is **2**. All four bodies are outside the walls at construction and remain outside after stepping. Applying the same recipe as an animated transition from a valid equal-wealth room instead leaves bodies airborne and `done === false` after 20 simulated seconds: they cannot land inside a wall narrower than themselves.

Calculate containing walls for the free-room placements before returning, including paired arrangements, and ensure placements fit those walls. Cover both initial construction and transition completion with wide bars and tall columns.

### 4 — P2: share links reproduce wealth but lose body proportions

Locations: [session.ts:564](stage/console/session.ts#L564), [session.ts:592](stage/console/session.ts#L592), [session.ts:1127](stage/console/session.ts#L1127).

Body aspect ratios depend on the scale in effect when each trade occurs and on `equalize`. A share link stores game commands and only the final recipe. `equalize` and historical recipe changes are absent. Loading replays every trade under the final recipe, so wealth matches while shapes and arrangements can change.

```ts
const s = new Session({ n: 4 });
s.run('fx off; trade 20; equalize');
const restored = Session.load(s.share());
```

The source's aspect ratios are `[1, 1, 1, 1]`; the restored values are approximately `[0.9937975, 1.152, 0.8753501, 0.9750021]`. No animation is pending in this reproduction. `fx off; log; trade 20; wealth` also diverges without using `equalize`, because the historical log scale is lost.

Serialize the canonical aspect state, or preserve all operations needed to derive it while maintaining the link's replay restrictions. Verify aspect ratios and compiled geometry as well as wealth in replay tests.

### 5 — P2: reset leaves a requested toss round queued

Location: [session.ts:897](stage/console/session.ts#L897).

`reset` clears game wealth and active transfers but leaves `queued`, `pairs`, and meeting progress intact. A queued toss round therefore executes after the reset even while playback is paused.

Reproduction: create a four-person session and run `fx trade toss; round; reset`. Immediately afterward there are zero trades but `queued === 1`. After 600 frames at `1/60`, there are **two trades**, history is `['round 1']`, and `playing` remains **false**.

Clear pending rounds and meeting state as part of reset before recompiling. Test resetting both before a meeting starts and during a meeting.

### 6 — P2: `until done` finishes before a queued toss round starts

Location: [session.ts:465](stage/console/session.ts#L465); script advancement precedes meeting handling in [session.ts:299](stage/console/session.ts#L299).

`settled` checks active transit and the current pose, but not queued work. Before a toss round starts, the old room is already settled and transit is empty. The script therefore advances past the wait immediately.

In a four-person session, run `fx trade toss; round; until done; say done`, then advance one frame. The script has ended and the caption is `done`, while **zero trades** have occurred and **one round remains queued**. A following pose command could also change the scene before the requested toss.

Treat queued rounds and pending meetings as unfinished work. Verify that the following script line runs only after the meeting and the final transfer have completed.

### 7 — P2: animated playback ignores the requested trade rate

Location: [session.ts:374](stage/console/session.ts#L374); related unconditional paths at [session.ts:317](stage/console/session.ts#L317) and [session.ts:397](stage/console/session.ts#L397).

The animated-round branch starts a round whenever transit is idle, without accumulating or checking the rate budget. The toss meeting and random synchronous paths have the same omission. Animation duration becomes the only throttle.

```ts
const s = new Session({ n: 4 });
s.run('play 0');
s.frame(1 / 60);
// s.game.trades === 2; s.history is ['round 1']
```

`play 0` is accepted and reports zero trades per second, yet a round executes. Low positive rates are also exceeded. Gate these branches on the requested budget; animations can impose an additional ceiling. Cover zero and low rates for each playback mode.

### 8 — P2: history compaction creates share links the loader rejects

Location: [session.ts:675](stage/console/session.ts#L675), versus round validation at [session.ts:925](stage/console/session.ts#L925).

`record()` merges both trades and rounds up to `MAX_TRADES`. A round command's bound is smaller: `floor(MAX_TRADES / floor(N / 2))`. Two valid commands can therefore become one invalid replay command.

With `n: 20`, run `fx off; rule beta 0; round 1000000; round 1`. Both round commands succeed, making 10,000,010 trades in total. History becomes `['rule beta 0', 'round 1000001']`. Loading the generated share link throws **`rounds must be in [0, 1000000]`**.

Use the command-specific bound when merging, or split oversized entries into legal chunks. Test compaction across the limit without requiring every replay entry to have originated as a single command.

### 9 — P2: explicit playback rate 1 is indistinguishable from the default rate in a link

Locations: [session.ts:568](stage/console/session.ts#L568), [session.ts:617](stage/console/session.ts#L617).

`share()` encodes both a default rate (`null`) and an explicit rate of one as `play=1`. Loading always interprets that value as the default N trades per second.

Create a four-person session, run `fx off; play 1`, and load its share link. `tradeRate` changes from **1** to **4**. The encoding has lost information before any frame runs.

Use a distinct default marker or a separate rate parameter. Round-trip `null`, zero, one, and an ordinary positive rate in the serializer tests.

### 10 — P2: push dragging can tunnel through small ground bodies

Locations: [stage.ts:703](stage/core/stage.ts#L703), [stage.ts:1132](stage/core/stage.ts#L1132), [stage.ts:1155](stage/core/stage.ts#L1155).

Dragging moves a body by up to `DRAG_SPEED * dt`, but contacts examine only the resulting positions. That displacement can exceed the width of a poor agent. A held body can pass completely through a neighbour and end non-overlapping, so contact resolution and overlap diagnostics miss the collision.

Reproduce with wealth `[1e-6, 1e-6, 0.999998]` in the `still` pose. Using lift drags, place the two small bodies at centres `(0.1, 1.5)` and `(0.11, 1.5)`. They are both 0.001 wide. Release them, grab the first in push mode, drag toward `(0.13, 1.5)`, and step `1/120` second. Its left edge moves from **0.0995** to **0.1295**, crossing the second body at **0.1095**. Both remain on the ground, the second body does not move, and no bump is reported.

Use swept collision handling or geometry-dependent movement limits. Verify the collision path, not only endpoint non-overlap, with strongly unequal wealth.

## Test gaps and repair order

The useful separation between game state, figures, physical motion, acts, and rendering is worth preserving. Most new failures occur where those modules meet: ledger correctness does not prove drawn area correctness, exact wealth replay does not prove shape replay, and a settled current pose does not mean scheduled work is complete.

First address the two animation geometry defects and free-room containment. Then make share state and queued-command lifecycle complete, followed by playback budgeting and swept dragging. Add targeted regressions at those boundaries; broad tests that merely run the demo or assert a final non-overlapping layout already pass with these failures present.
